# Open Bucket Coach

1. Tap **https://codespaces.new/levigrammer21/BucketCoach?quickstart=1**.
2. Tap **Create codespace** and wait for setup. This installs the app's browser.
3. If Bucket Coach doesn't open itself, open **Ports** → **3000** → **Open in Browser**.
4. Tap **Connect GameChanger** and sign in inside that window.
5. Tap **Scout a link**, paste a team/game share link, then tap **Start scouting**.

If the scout says it needs sign-in, tap **Finish sign-in & resume**. The account must be able to view the player's box scores in GameChanger. Codespaces is free only within your account's allowance; stop it when you're done.

The integration's live GameChanger sign-in/extraction still needs a successful test. Automated app-to-server tests passed with fixture data; those aren't a substitute for the live account test.
